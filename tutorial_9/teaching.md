# Tutorial 9 Teaching Guide

## Preparation

- Read the [official tutorial material](https://pages.github.students.cs.ubc.ca/cpsc317/site/tutorials/8/).
- Read `tutorial_9` in the [shared Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1gN4XpJVkrRdUYh8Xq5RmTiFIr8IjG44e?usp=sharing).
- Read the PA4 STCP specification on PrairieLearn.

## Notes

- Let students try the STCP Spec activity first, then review the five blue cases on Slide 17 together: 1, 2, 3, 5, and 6. The slides cover only these cases; the others are similar or extensions.
- The STCP Spec activity diagrams were created by past TAs and taken from the [2023WT2 Tutorial](past_slides/2023wt2.pdf). I asked previous TAs so I could credit the diagrams properly, but could not identify the original author.
